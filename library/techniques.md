# Ideation techniques

Pick the 2-3 that fit the channel. Every technique still has to borrow an outlier's psychology (step 3) and pass the
"anyone test" (could another channel post it unchanged? then it's not specific enough).
The examples only show the *shape* of each technique in two different niches; they are illustrations, not proof.

| Technique | How | Example (coding / tech) | Example (another niche) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Audience questions** | Mine comments on the creator's and the outliers' videos for questions; each good question is a video | "Why does my code work on my laptop but not on the server?" | (cooking) "Why does my bread never rise like yours?" |
| **Contrarian take** | Take a widely held belief in the niche and show where it's wrong (with proof) | "Python isn't slow. Your loop is." | (fitness) "Crunches don't burn belly fat" |
| **Myth vs reality** | A popular myth, tested | "We tested if closing apps saves battery" | (skincare) "We tested if a $90 serum beats a $9 one" |
| **Comparison / versus** | Two things the audience is choosing between, judged with a clear winner | "$300 laptop vs $3,000 laptop: same code, timed" | (travel) "$50 a day in Tokyo vs $50 a day in Paris" |
| **Challenge** | A constraint + stakes + a deadline | "I built an app using only a phone in 24 hours" | (finance) "I lived on $10 a day for a month: the receipts" |
| **Transformation** | Before → after with the process | "My 2 FPS game to 60 FPS in 5 fixes" | (DIY) "A $40 chair from the street, restored in 5 steps" |
| **Imagine you're…** | Put the viewer inside the system (second person) | "Imagine you're a password. Here's your worst day." | (health) "Imagine you're a glucose molecule after one soda" |
| **Metaphor world** | Explain a mechanism as a place or story | "RAM is a hotel and Chrome is the worst guest" | (finance) "Compound interest is a snowball rolling downhill" |
| **Hidden mechanism** | Something everyone uses daily, and the surprising thing inside it | "What actually happens when you press Enter in a URL bar" | (business) "How a free airport lounge makes money off you" |
| **Stakes story** | A real event told through its consequence, present tense | "A software glitch lost this company $440 million in 45 minutes" (Knight Capital, 2012) | (business) a real company failure told through the one decision that caused it |
| **Trend + niche** | A trending topic seen through the channel's lens | "The new AI phone feature, explained by what the chip does" | (fitness) a viral workout trend, judged by a physio |
| **Series / format** | A repeatable frame with endless episodes | "Explaining X like you're 5, then like you're a senior engineer" | (cooking) "One ingredient, three price points, blind-tasted" |
| **Ranked list with a twist** | A list where the order itself is the story | "Every sorting algorithm, ranked by how long it would take to sort the Earth" | (travel) "Every airline's carry-on rule, ranked by how much it costs you" |
| **What-if twist (use sparingly)** | Only with a specific, new twist an outlier proves; generic "what if" is the most overused AI idea | "What if your PC ran at the speed of light?" only if the twist is new | (any niche) the same rule applies |
