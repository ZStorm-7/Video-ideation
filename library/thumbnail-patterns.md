# Thumbnail patterns

One clear focal point, readable at phone size, high contrast, ≤ 4 words of text. The text adds to the title (never
repeats it word for word) but shares its key word. Shorts: the thumbnail is usually a frame you pick in the app, so the
most dramatic frame of the video is the thumbnail: plan that frame.

| Pattern | When | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Face + emotion + object** | creator on camera | shocked face pointing at a smoking laptop |
| **Before / after** | transformation, versus | blurry vs sharp, split down the middle |
| **The one object, huge** | hidden mechanism, explainer | a giant RAM stick with a tiny city on it |
| **Big number** | number-shock hooks | "$440,000,000" over a crashing stock chart |
| **Arrow / circle on the detail** | "look at this" reveals | red circle on one line of code |
| **Character in a situation** | animation / faceless channels | the channel's mascot sweating in front of a red ERROR screen |
| **Contrast colours** | any | warm subject on a cool background, or the reverse |

Concept format for the output: image (subject, action, setting, emotion) + text (≤ 4 words) + colour note.
