# Why it works (and what's only opinion)

| Rule | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Start from outliers, not a blank chat | Outlier-driven ideation is the core method of top YouTube strategists (e.g. Paddy Galloway, who has worked with MrBeast): study videos that beat their channel's normal views, especially in adjacent niches, and remix the format. A blank "viral ideas" prompt returns the same ideas to everyone. |
| 10x and 10,000-subscriber minimum | A creator heuristic, not a YouTube rule: 10x filters for real breakouts; the 10k floor removes tiny channels whose spikes are luck. |
| Packaging first (title + thumbnail before the video) | MrBeast's production guide names click-through rate, average view duration and average view percentage as the core metrics, and says CTR dictates what gets made. YouTube's own A/B "Test & compare" ranks thumbnails/titles by watch time. |
| Original, specific angles | YouTube's monetization policy on "inauthentic content" (renamed from "repetitious", July 2025) targets mass-produced, near-identical videos channel-wide. |
| First seconds matter | YouTube Studio reports "viewed vs swiped away" for Shorts and the share of viewers still watching at 30 s for long videos. |
| No ideal length; no shadowbans | YouTube staff say there's no ideal Short length and that early plateaus are the system testing a video, not a shadowban. |
| Demand via autocomplete | A free signal: autocomplete reflects real searches, but it is not a volume number. Use a keyword tool for volumes. |
