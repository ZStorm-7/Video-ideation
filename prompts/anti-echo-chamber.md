# The anti-echo-chamber prompt (step 4)

The agent runs this itself. It also works pasted into any free AI chat (paste the transcripts where marked).

```
Here is my channel profile and 5 outlier videos. Each got at least 10 times more views than its channel's subscribers
or its usual views, from channels with 10,000+ subscribers, in the last [7 / 30] days.

MY CHANNEL: [paste <notes>/profile.md, or: niche, audience, format, tone, what worked, what flopped]

OUTLIERS:
1. [title] - [link] - [views] views, [subs] subs, [ratio]x
   [paste transcript, or describe the first 30 seconds]
2. ...
5. ...

Don't give me generic ideas. Study only these 5 videos: the hook in the first 1.5-3 seconds, the story structure, the
pacing, the packaging (title + thumbnail), and why a stranger kept watching.

Then give me 12 new video ideas for MY channel that use the same psychology, on angles none of these 5 videos
covered and I haven't made (my past titles: [paste]). Nothing you would give anyone who asked for "viral video ideas".
No generic "what if" or "history facts" ideas unless you can name the specific twist that makes them new.

For each idea: the angle in one sentence, which outlier's psychology it borrows and why, the technique used, Short or
long-form, the first sentence spoken, and one line on why it fits my channel specifically.
```

## Variants
- **New creator (no channel yet):** replace MY CHANNEL with the chosen niche, positioning line and constraints.
- **Series mode:** "…give me 3 repeatable series formats instead, each with 5 episode ideas."
- **Backlog remix:** "…here are 10 ideas from my backlog: upgrade the 3 strongest using these outliers' psychology."
