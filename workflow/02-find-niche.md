# 2. Find (or change) the niche

For new creators, or anyone who asked to change direction.

## 2a. Viral niche or personal niche? **[ASK]**
Explain both, specifically, then let them choose:

| | **Viral niche** (built for what's trending) | **Personal niche** (built on your interests and skills) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Formats and topics that are exploding right now (e.g. AI explainers, Roblox stories, "how it's made" Shorts) | What you know or love (your job, hobby, skill, story) |
| Pros | Fast growth possible; lots of outliers to learn from; clear demand | You never run out of ideas; harder to copy; trust and loyalty; easier to monetize with products/sponsors that fit you |
| Cons | Crowded; trends die; you compete with copycats; burnout if you don't care about it | Slower start; you must prove demand; smaller ceiling if the niche is tiny |
| Best for | Shorts-first channels, faceless/animation channels, testing quickly | Long-form, creators who want a brand, experts |
| The check | Is it still growing (recent outliers in the last 30 days)? | Is there demand (people search it; the biggest channels in it have 100k+ subscribers)? |

Middle path worth suggesting: a **personal niche with a viral format** (your topic, told in a format that's working now).

## 2b. Narrow it down
Ask 3-5 quick questions (interests/skills, what they could make 50 videos about, who they want to help or entertain,
on camera or not, Shorts or long). Propose **3 candidate niches**, each with: a one-line positioning ("X for Y"), the
3 biggest channels in it with subscriber counts (demand ceiling), how crowded it is, one example angle.
If `niches/` has a pack for a candidate, read it for the adjacent niches and the overdone list; check the channel sizes
and crowding with a real search, not from the pack.

## 2c. Show 3 videos to study **[ASK]** pick a niche
For the chosen niche, recommend the **3 best videos to study right now**: recent, high views relative to the channel
(outliers when possible), from channels with 10k+ subscribers, ideally from 3 different channels. For each: link, why it
worked (hook, angle, packaging), what to copy (structure, pacing, style: never the topic).

Save the chosen niche, the positioning line and the 3 study videos to `<notes>/profile.md`.
