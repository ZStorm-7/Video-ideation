# Niche pack: business, entrepreneurship, side projects, career
- **Status:** unverified starting hypothesis. Step 3 outliers override anything here. Be careful with income claims: show proof or don't say it.
- **Adjacent niches to mine for outliers:** personal finance, tech and software, marketing and branding, productivity, case studies of famous companies, creator economy, small-business and trades.
- **Search keywords that surface outliers:** "how [company] actually makes money", "I started", "why [brand] failed", "case study", "the business of", "mistakes".
- **Formats that work here now (to test):** a company's business model explained through one product; a small business teardown with real numbers; "I tried X for 90 days" with the results; a failure post-mortem; a day in a real business.
- **Overdone (avoid unless you have a twist):** "5 habits of billionaires", screenshots of income with no method, "quit your job" hooks, dropshipping/crypto hype.
- **Hooks that fit:** hidden mechanism ("How a free product makes $2B"), number shock, stakes story, contrarian.
- **Thumbnail conventions:** a recognizable brand object, one big number, an arrow on the surprising detail, a before/after chart. Faces help when the creator is the credibility.
- **Example angles (not topics):** "How a $2 vending-machine snack is priced at $4.50 (the margin, line by line)" · "Why this local bakery survived when the chain next door closed".
