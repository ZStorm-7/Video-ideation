# Niche pack: gaming
- **Adjacent niches to mine for outliers:** game dev, speedrunning, lore/story channels, tech hardware, internet culture.
- **Search keywords that surface outliers:** "secret", "hidden", "lore", "speedrun", "100 days", "tier list", "update".
- **Formats that work here now:** challenges with a constraint; hidden mechanics; lore told as a story; "I tested the myth"; update reactions within 24 h.
- **Overdone:** plain gameplay with music; generic top 10 without a hook; clickbait "secret" with no payoff.
- **Hooks that fit:** demo first (show the glitch/trick), story (drop into the moment), challenge stakes.
- **Thumbnail conventions:** the game's most recognizable element + a face/character reaction + a big number or arrow.
- **Example angles:** "I beat the game using only the worst item" · "This 10-year-old glitch still works" · "The lore mistake nobody noticed".
