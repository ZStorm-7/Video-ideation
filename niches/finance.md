# Niche pack: personal finance, investing, money
- **Status:** unverified starting hypothesis. Step 3 outliers override anything here. Rules differ by country: no personalized advice, no guaranteed returns, disclose sponsors and affiliate links.
- **Adjacent niches to mine for outliers:** side hustles and business, productivity, real estate, career and salary, consumer protection and scams, economics explainers, minimalism and frugal living.
- **Search keywords that surface outliers:** "how much", "the real cost of", "mistakes", "what they don't tell you", "explained simply", "I tracked", "salary".
- **Formats that work here now (to test):** the real numbers behind a common purchase; a month of tracked spending; a scam or fee explained step by step; a rule of thumb tested on real data; "what I'd do with $X at age Y".
- **Overdone (avoid unless you have a twist):** "how to become a millionaire", generic "passive income ideas", get-rich-quick hooks without a mechanism, stock-pick hype.
- **Hooks that fit:** number shock (the true cost), contrarian ("Your emergency fund is too big"), story with stakes, you-focused ("You're paying this fee right now").
- **Thumbnail conventions:** one big number, a receipt or statement with a circle on the surprising line, a before/after balance, a calm face (urgency reads as scammy here).
- **Example angles (not topics):** "What a $4/day coffee costs over 30 years, with real index returns" · "The 3 fees hiding in my pension statement".
