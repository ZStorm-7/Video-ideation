# Niche pack: education / explainers
- **Adjacent niches to mine for outliers:** science, history-as-story, psychology, money, how-it's-made, documentaries.
- **Search keywords that surface outliers:** "explained", "why", "how", "truth about", "what happens if", "in 60 seconds".
- **Formats that work here now:** one surprising question answered visually; myth busting; "you've been doing X wrong"; a mechanism shown step by step.
- **Overdone:** dry lists of facts; history facts with no stakes; "did you know" with nothing surprising.
- **Hooks that fit:** question the viewer can't answer, paradox, you-focused ("You've done this a thousand times").
- **Thumbnail conventions:** the one object, huge; before/after; a question mark only if the image is the question.
- **Example angles:** "Why you can't tickle yourself" · "Why you forget what you studied by tomorrow" · "What your brain does in the first 10 seconds of a test".
