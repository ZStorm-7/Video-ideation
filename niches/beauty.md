# Niche pack: beauty, skincare, fashion, style
- **Status:** unverified starting hypothesis. Step 3 outliers override anything here. Skincare and ingredient claims need a source; disclose gifted products and sponsors.
- **Adjacent niches to mine for outliers:** lifestyle and "get ready with me", thrifting and DIY fashion, health and dermatology, shopping and consumer testing, hair care, personal care for men, costume and cosplay.
- **Search keywords that surface outliers:** "dermatologist reacts", "I tried", "dupe", "worth it", "mistakes", "routine", "before and after", "on a budget".
- **Formats that work here now (to test):** an expensive product vs its dupe; a routine simplified to 3 steps; a viral trend tested honestly; a "mistakes that age you / ruin your makeup" fix; a wardrobe built under a budget.
- **Overdone (avoid unless you have a twist):** unfocused "get ready with me" with no angle, "my morning routine", haul videos without a verdict, generic product reviews.
- **Hooks that fit:** contrarian ("Stop layering that many products"), demo first (the result before the steps), you-focused, number shock (cost per use).
- **Thumbnail conventions:** a clear before/after split, the product beside the result, high-contrast close-up of the face or item, ≤ 3 words.
- **Example angles (not topics):** "I used the viral $8 serum and the $90 one for 30 days; here's the photo test" · "The 3-product routine a dermatologist told me to cut to".
