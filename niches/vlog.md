# Niche pack: vlogs / lifestyle
- **Adjacent niches to mine for outliers:** challenges, travel, food, productivity, money, relationships, documentary-style personal stories.
- **Search keywords that surface outliers:** "I tried", "for 30 days", "day in the life", "living on", "my honest", "what I wish I knew".
- **Formats that work here now:** constraint challenges; transformations over time; honest result reveals; "the cheapest vs most expensive".
- **Overdone:** day-in-the-life with no stakes or arc; "my morning routine" without a twist.
- **Hooks that fit:** story (start at the dramatic moment), result first ("I lost $500 doing this"), contrarian.
- **Thumbnail conventions:** your face with a strong emotion + the one object/place that sells the story; ≤ 3 words.
- **Example angles:** "I lived on $5 a day in New York for a week" · "I tried the viral productivity method for 30 days: honest results".
