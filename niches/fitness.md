# Niche pack: fitness, health, sport
- **Status:** unverified starting hypothesis. Step 3 outliers override anything here. Health claims need a source: never invent a number or a medical promise.
- **Adjacent niches to mine for outliers:** sports science, nutrition myth-busting, physical therapy and mobility, productivity and habits, running and cycling, home workouts, transformation stories.
- **Search keywords that surface outliers:** "science of", "what happens if you", "I did X for 30 days", "mistakes", "form check", "doctor reacts", "beginner".
- **Formats that work here now (to test):** a 30-day challenge with a measurable result; a popular belief tested against a study; a common exercise mistake with a fix on screen; a "what a physio sees" explainer; a beginner's first week.
- **Overdone (avoid unless you have a twist):** "full-body workout, no equipment" with no angle, "I got abs in 7 days", before/after photos without a process, miracle-supplement claims.
- **Hooks that fit:** contrarian ("Crunches don't burn belly fat"), you-focused ("You've been doing squats wrong"), number shock, demo first (the fix before the explanation).
- **Thumbnail conventions:** body part or movement clearly shown, a red mark on the mistake, a before/after split, big number, one face with a strong expression.
- **Example angles (not topics):** "What happens to your body if you walk 10,000 steps for a month (a physio reads the data)" · "The one setup mistake behind most lower-back pain in squats".
