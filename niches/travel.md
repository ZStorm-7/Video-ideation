# Niche pack: travel, places, culture
- **Status:** unverified starting hypothesis. Step 3 outliers override anything here. Check prices, visa and safety facts on the day you publish: they go stale fast.
- **Adjacent niches to mine for outliers:** vlogs and lifestyle, history and geography explainers, food and street food, budget living, expat life, language learning, photography, aviation and trains.
- **Search keywords that surface outliers:** "what it's really like", "I spent X in", "mistakes tourists make", "hidden", "cheapest", "how much does it cost to", "why is [place]".
- **Formats that work here now (to test):** a fixed budget in an expensive place; the real cost of a trip, line by line; a tourist trap vs the local alternative; a place explained by one odd rule or fact; a constraint trip (one train line, 24 hours).
- **Overdone (avoid unless you have a twist):** "top 10 places to visit", generic "travel guide to [country]", drone montages with music only, "I went to the most dangerous place".
- **Hooks that fit:** number shock (the real price), contrarian ("Skip the famous one"), story (drop into the odd moment), you-focused.
- **Thumbnail conventions:** the place's most recognizable shape, a face reacting, a price or time on screen, saturated colour, a circle on the surprising detail.
- **Example angles (not topics):** "I spent exactly $50 a day in [expensive city]; here's the receipt" · "Why [famous street] has a rule locals pretend not to know".
