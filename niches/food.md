- **Search keywords that surface outliers:** "why does", "the science of", "I tried", "cheapest vs most expensive", "restaurant secret", "mistakes you're making", "how it's made".
- **Formats that work here now (to test):** one technique tested head to head; the same dish at three price points; a restaurant trick explained for home; a mistake that ruins a common dish; a tight constraint (one pan, 5 ingredients, a $5 budget).

- **Hooks that fit:** demo first (the finished plate, then "most people ruin step 2"), contrarian ("Stop rinsing your rice"), number shock (cost per serving).

- **Example angles (not topics):** "Why restaurant scrambled eggs taste different (it's not the butter)" · "I tested 5 ways to cook rice; only one wasn't mushy" · "The $3 version of a $30 dish, blind-tasted".
