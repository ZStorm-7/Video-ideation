# Niche pack: tech, coding, computer science
- **Adjacent niches to mine for outliers:** science explainers, "how it's made", engineering, gadgets, productivity, gaming tech, true-crime-style security stories.
- **Search keywords that surface outliers:** "how it works", "explained", "what happens when", "truth about", "hidden feature", "engineering".
- **Formats that work here now:** hidden mechanism of an everyday device; myth vs reality; security stakes stories; "I built X in Y hours"; animated metaphor worlds.
- **Overdone:** "what if the internet disappeared", "top 10 coding tips", "the history of the computer", generic AI news.
- **Hooks that fit:** bold claim about the viewer's own device ("Your phone is lying to you."), number shock (real incidents), demo first.
- **Thumbnail conventions:** one device or object huge, a red alert/error, a face or character reacting; ≤ 3 words.
- **Example angles:** "Why your phone charges slower after 80 %" · "The first 60 seconds after your account gets hacked" · "Python isn't slow, your loop is".
